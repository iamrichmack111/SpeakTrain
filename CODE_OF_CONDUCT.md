# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SpeakTrain a harassment-free experience regardless of background, identity, experience, language level, or dialect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Treat native speakers, learners, dialects, accents, and transliteration choices with care.
- Correct language material with evidence and without belittling contributors.
- Protect learner privacy and never publish recordings, grades, databases, or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential exposure, or deliberate publication of private learner data is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy follows the intent of Contributor Covenant 2.1 in a concise project-specific form.
