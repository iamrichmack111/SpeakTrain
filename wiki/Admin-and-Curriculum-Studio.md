# Admin and Curriculum Studio

## User management

Administrators can add students/admins, reset passwords, delete other users, inspect 30-day performance, and export grades with strengths, weaknesses, and teaching tips.

## Curriculum Studio

Add custom phrases to any course without editing JSON. Fields support:

- language, course, and module;
- English meaning and target text;
- transliteration;
- formal/informal forms;
- speaking to a man/woman;
- natural response and response transliteration;
- teaching notes.

The Vocabulary Editor adds searchable trilingual words, categories, parts of speech, pronunciation, cognate labels, and transfer notes.

Deletion removes custom curriculum and its review schedule, but historical score rows remain available for audit context.
