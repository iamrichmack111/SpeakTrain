# Conjugation Drill

Choose Spanish or Syrian Arabic and present or past tense. The prompt combines a person with a verb; select the correctly inflected form.

## Arabic persons

- I: `ana`
- you, man: `inta`
- you, woman: `inti`
- he: `huwwe`
- she: `hiyye`
- we: `niḥna`

Do not memorize the table only by rows. Mix persons, say the pronoun and verb together, and then add a compatible complement in the Sentence Lab.
