# Courses and Mastery

## Course sequence

1. **Social Foundations:** introductions, identity, locations, and first meetings.
2. **Building Connection:** contact information, appointments, compliments, invitations, and plans.
3. **Curious Conversation:** personality, values, interests, and follow-up questions.
4. **Storytelling:** past events, sequence markers, complications, reactions, and resolutions.

## Mastery states

| State | Meaning | Learner action |
|---|---|---|
| New | Not yet tested | Learn meaning and sound |
| Learning | One useful retrieval | Review the next day |
| Familiar | Recalled across sessions | Vary gender, register, or details |
| Strong | Reliable delayed recall | Use inside conversation |
| Mastered | Repeated high-quality recall | Maintain on long intervals |

Course completion counts Strong and Mastered phrases. XP level alone does not prove course mastery.
