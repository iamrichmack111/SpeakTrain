# Guided Conversation

Guided Conversation presents a target-language prompt and several possible replies. Choose the most natural response, then listen to and shadow the full answer.

## Three-pass method

1. Understand the prompt without translating word by word.
2. Select or predict the response.
3. Replace one detail—person, time, place, reason, or activity—and speak a new response.

Use this page to bridge recognition and spontaneous conversation before attempting longer OPI responses.
