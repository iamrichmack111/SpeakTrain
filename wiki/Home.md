# SpeakTrain Wiki

SpeakTrain is a local-first Spanish and Syrian/Levantine Arabic speaking trainer. It combines adaptive review, gender-aware forms, formal/informal registers, vocabulary bridges, conjugation, guided conversation, storytelling, progress reports, and OPI-style practice.
