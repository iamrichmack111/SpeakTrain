# OPI Simulator

The simulator practices unrehearsed production across warm-up, past narration, role-play, and complication/opinion prompts.

## How to practice

1. Respond for 30–90 seconds without reading a prepared script.
2. Give a direct answer, supporting detail, and example.
3. For narration, establish setting, sequence, complication, and resolution.
4. For opinion, state a position, explain why, and address an alternative.

The displayed ILR-style result is a practice estimate based on response features. Only an authorized test and certified raters can issue an official rating.
