# Phrase Practice

Phrase Practice combines meaning, target script, transliteration, cipher fading, audio, recording, and recognition feedback.

## Controls

- Choose language, course, situation, and phrase.
- Switch formal/informal style where available.
- Select whether the person addressed is a man or woman.
- Use **Hear it**, then **Cipher**, then record from memory.
- Read the natural response and its transliteration after producing the prompt.

## Score interpretation

The percentage is a transcription match, not a phoneme-level accent grade. Review Missing and Extra/Different words. For Arabic, confirm gender endings by reading the transliteration even when unvocalized spelling looks similar.
