# Proficiency Roadmap

SpeakTrain defines operational goals so learners know what “progress” means. These are internal practice benchmarks, not official CEFR, ACTFL, OPI, or ILR ratings.

## Stage 1 — Survival foundation

- 40 Strong/Mastered words or phrases.
- At least 80% vocabulary and conjugation accuracy.
- Handle introductions, locations, household requests, and appointment basics.
- Distinguish Arabic `-ak` and `-ik` forms when prompted.

## Stage 2 — Functional interaction

- 100 Strong/Mastered items across at least three courses.
- At least 85% phrase recognition and recall.
- Make plans, exchange contact information, ask follow-up questions, and repair misunderstandings.
- Use present tense across six persons and produce common past forms.

## Stage 3 — Sustained conversation

- 200 Strong/Mastered items and 80%+ conjugation accuracy over 30 days.
- Speak for 2–3 minutes with a beginning, details, complication, and conclusion.
- Maintain roughly 100–140 speech-active WPM without sacrificing accuracy.
- Switch Spanish `tú/usted` and Syrian/formal Arabic appropriately.

## Stage 4 — OPI readiness practice

- Sustain 10–15 minutes across narration, role-play, complication, and opinion.
- Support answers with reasons, examples, comparisons, and time frames.
- Recover when a word is missing by paraphrasing.
- Complete multiple unrehearsed recordings on different days.

## Weekly advancement rule

Advance course difficulty only when delayed recall remains at or above 80%. If accuracy falls, reduce speed, return to shadowing, and allow the adaptive scheduler to rebuild the memory trace.
