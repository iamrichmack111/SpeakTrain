# Progress and Reports

Progress reports phrase attempts, average recognition match, best score, valid WPM samples, vocabulary accuracy, conjugation accuracy, weak phrases, and recommendations.

## Printable reports

- **Daily:** compact immediate review sheet.
- **Weekly:** balanced practice and grade summary.
- **Monthly:** broader vocabulary and priority review list.

Each report includes strengths, weaknesses, training tips, target script, and transliteration. Print it or use the browser’s Save as PDF option.
