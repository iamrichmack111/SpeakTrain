# Vocabulary and Sentence Lab

## Searchable vocabulary

Filter by category or cognates. Each row provides English, Spanish, Spanish sound guidance, Syrian Arabic, Arabic transliteration, part of speech, and a transfer note.

## Conjugation workshop

Select a language, verb, and tense to compare every person. Arabic forms explicitly distinguish masculine and feminine second person.

## Sentence builder

Choose subject, verb, compatible complement, time, negative, and question settings. The builder filters complements by verb to reduce unnatural combinations. Listen, copy, and then create a second sentence without the controls.
